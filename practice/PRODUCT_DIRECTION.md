# Product direction after reviewing Arini

Sources reviewed September 2026: [Arini home](https://www.arini.ai/) and [AI receptionist](https://www.arini.ai/ai-receptionist-page). These are vendor descriptions, not independent proof of performance.

Arini presents a complete operational workflow: inbound reception, scheduling rules tailored to each practice, outbound patient engagement, online requests, staff tasks, and visibility into outcomes across locations. Its product page also emphasizes onboarding and ongoing support. The useful lesson is to make the receptionist dependable inside a clinic's existing processes; a similar-looking dashboard alone does not create that product.

## Implemented foundation

Single-clinic account isolation, Google/Apple integration awaiting credentials, task follow-up, paginated history, truthful calendar failures, and a public request form that does not mislabel requests as bookings. New calls retain operational outcomes rather than raw recordings while consent remains unfinished.

## Next release gates

1. Pick one launch country and one dental software integration. Agree appointment types, durations, practitioner resources, emergency handling and handoff rules with pilot clinics. Replace manually approved calendar slots with authoritative PMS availability, transactional booking and reconciliation.
2. Activate real OAuth, provision each staff member, add owner-managed invitations and an access audit trail. Validate backups and restoration. For chains, introduce organisations and location-level permissions before restoring any selector.
3. Complete provider contracts, hosting/transfer assessment, clinic privacy notices, retention policy and a tested consent/withdrawal flow for the launch country. Disabling local recordings does not establish legal compliance or remove provider processing.
4. Run supervised calls covering interruptions, accents, ambiguous dates, urgent symptoms, provider outages, duplicate requests and calendar conflicts. Define measurable acceptance thresholds with the clinic and review each failed handoff.
5. Measure answered calls, confirmed bookings, pending requests, handoff completion and booking failures. Link new booking events to their call IDs; avoid backfilling an invented conversion rate for legacy history.
6. Add reminders/reactivation only after authoritative patient status, opt-out handling, contact permissions and delivery tracking exist. Then build campaign controls that reflect actual sending behaviour.

Use our own brand, interface copy and evidence. Do not reuse Arini testimonials, customer logos or revenue claims as ours. Full Arini feature parity is not claimed by this release.
